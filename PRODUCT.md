## Users

Primary users are friends, family, and people who discover AnaLu's work through Instagram or word of mouth. They are browsing a personal portfolio — evaluating craft, style, and personal fit. Many arrive pre-disposed to beauty from social feeds; they are evaluating examples and emotional fit as much as anything.

## Product Purpose

Made By AnaLu is a personal portfolio for AnaLu's sweet-table decoration hobby. She handcrafts decoration for events such as birthdays, baby showers, weddings, and romantic occasions, working from a theme she chooses or one a client requests. The site exists to introduce AnaLu as a person, showcase her work, and make it easy for interested people to reach out and connect. Success means the visitor meets AnaLu, sees the quality and care in her work, and gets in touch (Instagram, WhatsApp, or word of mouth). This is not a commercial service — it's a creative portfolio for exposure and community.

## Positioning

Everything AnaLu makes is handcrafted and deeply personalized: each piece is oriented to what the guest of honor likes and to where the event takes place, made with great care. The claim a neighboring service could not copy is the one-of-one, made-with-care personalization per event and per client — not a catalog of rented props.

## Operating Context

AnaLu operates in Madrid and its surrounding area. Clients are mostly friends or people who find her through Instagram and word of mouth; some reach her on WhatsApp as friends-of-a-friend. Browsing happens on phones and social feeds, so the site must feel native to that context and make it obvious how to get in touch.

## Product Principles

- Show the work first: real photos of real events carry the pitch; copy supports, never overwhelms, the imagery.
- Personalization is the product: every surface should communicate that each piece is made for one event, one guest of honor, one place.
- Make reaching out effortless: contact paths (Instagram, WhatsApp, direct message) must be impossible to miss and match how her clients actually reach her.
- Respect the maker's craft: a handcrafted, warm tone; no template corporate feel.
- Honor the real audience: Spanish first, with English and Portuguese — never a broken or half-translated experience in any language.
