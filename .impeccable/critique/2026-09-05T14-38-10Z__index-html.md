## Design Specificity Verdict

Services section is text-only (generic), social links are placeholders, palette drift dilutes the token system. ~7/10 specific.

## Priority Issues

- **P0 — Contact form is a facade.** Submit handler silently discards data, shows false confirmation. Total data loss for primary conversion.
- **P0 — Conversion channels are placeholders.** WhatsApp is wa.me/34 (no number), Instagram is generic. Both dead.
- **P1 — `.services-dense` has no CSS rule.** All 6 service cards stack single-column on every viewport.
- **P1 — Accessibility: gallery & blog unreachable.** Click-only divs/articles, no tabindex/role/aria-label, no focus trap, no visible focus ring.
- **P1 — Contrast failure.** #B08D86 on cream/white ≈3:1, fails WCAG AA (4.5:1). Gold #C9A962 on cream ≈2.25:1 also fails.
